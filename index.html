The actual problem

A campus calendar isn’t one calendar. It’s a small, unruly ecosystem.

Events come from departments, student groups, room systems, Google calendars, people with five minutes between meetings, and people who absolutely will not read a 40-page training guide. LiveWhale is built for that reality.

01

Let people own their part.

Departments and groups can manage their own events, while central teams keep standards, permissions, promotion, and oversight where they belong.

02

Bring the other calendars with you.

Pull in events from the systems people already use. Share them across groups. Publish them wherever they need to appear. Stop retyping the same event four times.

03

Make events worth finding.

Images, locations, categories, related content, featured events, flexible displays—the calendar becomes an editorial tool, not a dumping ground.

04

Don’t make your website look like our software.

LiveWhale works with your CMS, your brand, and your front end. Your calendar should look unmistakably like your institution.
